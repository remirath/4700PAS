lasermodel.m is a Distributed Feedback (DBR) laser model.

INCLUDED FILES:' wspace.m
		 SourceFct.m
		 lasermodel.m '

Upon running lasermodel.m, information such as electric field strength, carrier densities,
photon densities, average carrier densities, and input/output waveforms over time are updated
live. When the simulation finishes, the transient response and frequency domain analysis are
displayed. A visualization of the grating coupling coefficient magnitude throughout the waveguide 
is also displayed for ease of use. 

CONTROL VARIABLES

lambda - sets the operating wavelength
LGain - control gain through waveguide
SPE - amount of  spontaneous emission
delta_f - DETUNING FREQUENCY
L - control length of waveguide
kappa_0 - COUPLING COEFFICIENT
I_on / I_off - drive current on / off
